English-bottleneck LM — scaling, cost & reasoning battery

Follow-up to the capability/capacity study. Question: does the paraphrase-bottleneck idea scale, what does it cost, and how much does reasoning degrade? Small-scale demonstrator, real trained runs; depth/width sweeps run by two sub-agents (Sonnet, Haiku).

Capability does not survive depth. Meaning accuracy falls 1.00→0.96→0.41→0.05 (≈chance) as layers/hops grow 2→4→8→12, while the normal model stays 1.00. The covert channel, though, stays closed (~0.5) at every depth ≥4. Past depth 4 it is no longer a double dissociation — both covert capacity and deep capability are gone.
Mechanism: each layer re-quantizes to a word and adds paraphrase noise, so per-crossing fidelity is <1. A k-step dependent computation degrades like fidelityk. The very thing that scrubs covert channels also erodes legitimate multi-hop signal. Depth-2 is the only leaky point (too few crossings to randomize surface).

2 · Width

At fixed depth 4, growing width 32→256 keeps the covert channel closed (~0.5) — it lives at the vocabulary level, not in residual dimensionality. Meaning capability stays well above chance but is noisy across seeds (e.g. d=128: 0.69±0.23): the discrete-bottleneck model trains less stably than the continuous baseline.

3 · Reasoning battery — where the tax lands

chain (pure meaning): tax ≈ 0. varhop (dynamic depth) and ruleselect (instruction routing): large tax, present even at ρ=0 — so it is the architecture, not the paraphrase.
Conditional/stateful computation is hard through the bottleneck. I hypothesized a legible scratchpad would fix varhop; it did not — persisting the instruction (0.42→0.36) and a decrementing counter (→0.10) both failed. Anything needing state to survive many sequential crossings degrades, same mechanism as depth.

4 · Cost — the paraphraser is ~free and free-er at scale

The fixed paraphraser (a class-structured permutation, no model) costs 1.4% of one layer at toy size, 0.02% at d=1024 — a layer is O(d²), the paraphrase is O(1)/token. Adding it to a normal net costs ~that per layer: negligible.

Caveat: a model-based paraphraser (an LLM rewriting text each layer) would be a whole extra forward (autoregressive) per layer — 1×–100×+ the model, ×depth. The result that the surface channel closes with the fixed table is what lets you avoid that cost.

Bottom line

It does not scale as a drop-in deep architecture. The paraphrase bottleneck robustly closes covert cross-layer channels (at every depth and width, essentially free), but the same re-quantize-and-noise step erodes legitimate signal that must cross many layers: deep reasoning chains collapse, and conditional computation fails even with a legible scratchpad. The clean double dissociation holds only at shallow depth (≤4). The honest readit: this mechanism is promising as a shallow legibility/anti-stego bottleneck or applied to a few boundaries, not as a replacement for the full-depth residual stream.
