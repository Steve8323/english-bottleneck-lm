Bottom line. The English-bottleneck LM matches a normal transformer on meaning-based reasoning at every paraphrase strength, while driving covert cross-layer channel capacity to chance — and the paraphraser that does it is a fixed permutation, ~72× cheaper than a layer. The only capability it loses is the ability to carry illegible (non-meaning) information across layers, which is the entire point.
